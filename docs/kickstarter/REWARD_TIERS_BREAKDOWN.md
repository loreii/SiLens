## Tier 1: SEED — $49

### "The Believer"

**What's Included:**
- Name in credits (GitHub README, website supporters page)
- Digital thank-you card (custom artwork)
- Early access to all development updates (private newsletter)
- Discord role: "Founding Supporter" (exclusive channel access)

**Rationale:**
- Entry-level tier for supporters who want to contribute but don't need hardware
- 100% margin — pure funding support
- Builds community and mailing list
- No fulfillment cost (digital only)

**Fulfillment:**
- Immediate: Discord invite, newsletter signup
- Month 1: Digital thank-you card delivered
- Ongoing: Development updates
- At launch: Name added to credits
