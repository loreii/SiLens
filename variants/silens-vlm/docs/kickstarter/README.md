## 💡 Key Messages (Revised for Honesty)

### Primary Pitch (DevKit)
> SiLens DevKit is your entry point to open-source AI hardware. Develop on our platform today with FPGA, upgrade to custom silicon when we get there. Help us prove the market exists.

### What We're NOT Claiming
- ❌ "100× faster than GPU" (that's the future ASIC)
- ❌ "Ships in 18 months" (ASIC timeline, not DevKit)
- ❌ "$100K covers everything" (it doesn't)

### What We ARE Claiming
- ✅ FPGA DevKit: 5-10× faster than CPU inference
- ✅ Same software interface as future ASIC
- ✅ Fully open source (RTL, PCB, software)
- ✅ Honest about what this phase delivers
- ✅ DevKit ships in 10-12 months

### The Honest Pitch
> "We're building toward an ASIC that's 100× faster than a GPU. This campaign funds Step 1: an FPGA DevKit that proves the architecture and builds our community. Back us if you want to be part of the journey."
